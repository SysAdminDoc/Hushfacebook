# Hushfacebook marketing source

The selected direction keeps the compact H geometry of Hushfeed but uses a Facebook-oriented blue and white palette. The first cyan and pink concepts remain under `candidates/` so the rejected direction isn't mistaken for unfinished work.

## Selected files

- `selected/icon-master.png`: generated icon source.
- `source/hero-background.png`: generated, text-free hero source.
- `scripts/build-marketing-assets.ps1`: deterministic typography, crops, icon sizes, lockups, and final exports.

## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand.

Create the final production icon for Hushfacebook, a Facebook privacy and feed-cleanup patch bundle and sister project to Hushfeed.

Input Image 1 is the Hushfeed icon. Borrow its compact rounded-square silhouette, bold geometric H monogram, stacked depth, and polished sibling-project character. Do not reuse its TikTok-like cyan and pink palette or copy its exact geometry.

Use royal blue `#1877F2` as the main color, deeper blue `#0B4DB8` and dark navy `#071B3D` for depth, and clean white `#F8FBFF` for the monogram. Use no cyan, teal, turquoise, pink, magenta, coral, red, or purple. Make the main letter a large white capital H from thick rounded geometric bars. Give it one subtle medium-blue offset layer toward the upper-left and one navy layer toward the lower-right. Add two small, identical royal-blue rounded-rectangle cuts across the center bar to suggest a cleaned social feed. Keep the H readable at 16 px.

Use flat vector-like construction and clean mathematical contours. Output a square RGBA PNG with transparent pixels outside the rounded-square corners. Include no words, extra letters, Facebook `f`, Meta infinity symbol, copied trademark geometry, watermark, or mockup.

## Hero prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.

Create the final wide 16:9 marketing hero background for Hushfacebook. Precise typography and the H icon will be added separately.

Use the approved Hushfacebook icon as the sole color reference. Use deep navy `#06152F` and ink blue `#071B3D` for the left background, royal blue `#1877F2` and medium blue `#0B5ED7` for the filtering boundary and useful cards, and pale ice blue `#DDEBFF` with white `#F8FBFF` for highlights. Use no cyan, teal, turquoise, pink, magenta, coral, red, or purple.

Reserve the full left 54 percent as calm, dark, low-detail negative space. On the right, show a generic social feed being cleaned. Translucent stacks of feed-card forms approach a bright blue filtering boundary. Distracting outer cards break into small blue-grey particles while four useful central cards pass through clean and crisp. Keep it minimal and technically credible. Include no text, logos, Facebook `f`, Meta symbol, recognizable Facebook interface, people, hands, device mockups, or transparent areas.
